About Lung Scan Analysis

Chest X-rays and CT scans are the primary tools for evaluating lung health. AI can help identify patterns that may indicate conditions like pneumonia, COPD, or lung nodules.

Common Respiratory Conditions

  • Pneumonia — bacterial or viral lung infection
  • COPD — chronic obstructive pulmonary disease
  • Asthma — airway inflammation and spasms
  • Lung cancer — abnormal cell growth in lungs
  • Tuberculosis — bacterial lung infection
This tool is for educational purposes only. Consult a certified pulmonologist for proper diagnosis.
